#######################################################################################
#  A. WHAT "AGENTS" ARE IN THIS PROJECT?                                               #
#######################################################################################

There are NO external agents and NO paid cloud AI services. Everything runs on this laptop
and is free. The project is one app made of these parts:

Part                     What it is                                 Port / place
-----------------------  -----------------------------------------  -------------------------
frontend                 the dashboard (Next.js) you use in Chrome   http://localhost:3000
backend                  the brain (NestJS): API, job queue,         :4000
                         approval rules, publishing
PostgreSQL               database (stories, characters, prompts,     :5432
                         versions, approvals...)
Redis                    job queue (BullMQ): one heavy job at a time :6379
media-service            images, voices, video editing (Python)      :8000
reference-service        reads reference reels (Agent Reach,         :8100
                         yt-dlp) for style research only
Ollama                   local AI that writes (qwen2.5:7b) and       :11434
                         looks at pictures (qwen2.5vl:3b)
ComfyUI                  local AI that draws pictures (DreamShaper   :8188
                         SD 1.5 + IP-Adapter) and, from Phase 4,
                         animates them (AnimateDiff)
Kokoro TTS               local AI voices (Hindi + English)           inside media-service
faster-whisper           speech-to-text for subtitle timing          inside media-service
FFmpeg                   cuts, camera motion, music mix, final MP4   inside media-service

The only "agent-like" tool is Agent Reach: it studies reference videos you add in the
Reference Library. It does NOT make reels.

Why it is slow: everything runs on the laptop CPU (no NVIDIA graphics card), and 16 GB RAM
holds only one AI model at a time, so the jobs run one after another.
A 1-minute reel takes about 1-1.5 hours. 5 reels a day = an overnight batch.


#######################################################################################
#  B. THE PROCESS: AI DOES EVERYTHING, I ONLY REVIEW AND UPLOAD                        #
#######################################################################################

  1. Settings -> Schedule: turn ON, set 5 reels per day and a start time (e.g. 01:00).
     (Or make one reel now: Content Studio -> "Generate reel".)
  2. The AI makes the reels on its own (section C).
  3. In the morning: Review Studio -> watch each reel.
       Approve        -> Publishing Center uploads it (YouTube / Instagram)
       Reject         -> it is never published
       Request changes-> the AI rewrites the story and makes a new version
       Regenerate scene (Review Studio -> Scenes) -> redraws just one bad scene
  4. NOTHING is ever published without my approval. The backend checks the approval in the
     database again right before every upload. A changed video needs a new approval.

TWO WAYS TO MAKE THE VIDEO PART (chosen 26-11-2026: Google Flow for real quality)
  Google Flow (recommended)  ReelForge writes the story, characters and one Veo prompt per
                             scene; I make each 8 s clip in Google Flow (my account, no extra
                             cost) and upload it; ReelForge assembles, adds music + subtitles,
                             checks, review, publish. Real animation + natural Hindi voices.
                             Step by step: RUNNING_NOTES.txt section 3a.
  Fully local (fallback)     Everything automatic on this laptop, but only still pictures with
                             camera motion and robotic Kokoro voices. Far below Flow quality.
  Why not automatic Flow: Flow has no public API. The paid Veo API could automate it later.

Story source (Content Studio, top of the page):
  AI story       the AI picks and writes the story itself (used by the daily schedule)
  My idea        I give a premise, the AI writes the full story
  My full story  I paste a whole story, the AI keeps what happens and only splits it into scenes
  Next episode   continues an earlier story with the same characters
Audience: 1-3 / 4-6 / 7-10 years / general (changes words, speed and emotional depth).

2. CHOOSING CHARACTERS
----------------------
About the Kahani page (aivnora.web.app/kahani.html):
  It is a static how-to guide, not an app. It has no character picker. Characters are
  just named in a text outline ("who - where - what is happening"). It has no
  per-character voices and says outright it has no lip-sync.
  Worth copying: its 5 visual style presets - Shadow Puppet, Madhubani folk, Chibi,
  Painterly, Stick-figure. These suit mythology and history stories.

Today the cast is fixed to the characters in Character Studio (Rabbit and Squirrel).

Planned:
  - Character collections in Character Studio, e.g. Kids, Ramayan, Mahabharat,
    Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj. Each character keeps its own reference images,
    appearance prompt and voice.
  - Cast picker when creating a story: choose a collection, tick the characters,
    mark the narrator and the main character.
  - Style presets per story or per collection.

Note: deities and historical figures need careful prompts (traditional iconography,
respectful depiction). These guidelines will be written into each collection.


3. VOICES AND LIP-SYNC
----------------------
Per-character voices: ALREADY WORK.
  Each dialogue line is spoken in that character's voice profile; narration uses the
  narrator voice. Engine: Kokoro TTS (English + a few Hindi voices).

Your own voice (voice cloning): possible for free, BUT most free cloning models are
  licensed for non-commercial use only. Matters if the app will be sold.

Lip-sync: NOT built.
  - Open-source options (Wav2Lip, SadTalker, LivePortrait) are built for human faces
    and do poorly on cartoon animals.
  - On this CPU-only laptop they take many minutes per second of video.
  - Several have non-commercial licences.
  Advice: skip lip-sync until there is an NVIDIA GPU. Until then, a cheap substitute is
  a "talking" effect - a gentle zoom/pulse on whichever character is speaking.

5. "HOW MANY TOKENS PER DAY?"
-----------------------------
Local models have NO token limit and NO per-token cost. The only limit is the
laptop's time (and electricity).

  - Measured: about 1 hour per reel, mostly image generation (~5 min per image).
  - 5 reels per day = ~5 hours -> works as an overnight batch.
  - About 10 reels per day is this laptop's ceiling.

More reels later:
  - Add an NVIDIA GPU (e.g. RTX 3060 12 GB): images drop from ~5 minutes to seconds,
    20-50 reels/day becomes realistic, and lip-sync / real animation become possible.
  - Or add a second machine as a worker - the job queue (BullMQ) already supports it.


6. SELLING THE APP AND GIVING DEMOS
-----------------------------------
Today it is a single-user app that runs on your own PC. Two ways to sell it:

A. Self-hosted (buyer runs it on their own PC)
   - Their models are free; no running costs for you.
   - They need decent hardware and their own Google and Meta app setup (as done here).
   - Least work.

B. Hosted SaaS (you run it for customers)
   - Models are still free to use, but YOU pay for GPU servers.
   - Needs: multiple users/workspaces, per-customer social accounts, billing and
     usage quotas.
   - Google must verify the app before other people can use the YouTube upload scope.
   - Meta requires App Review + business verification before publishing to other
     people's Instagram accounts.

Licence check needed before selling:
   - Appear fine for commercial use: Kokoro, faster-whisper, DreamShaper.
   - Need verifying: the 3B vision model's licence may be research-only.
   - Downloading Instagram reels with yt-dlp breaks Instagram's terms - a risk in a
     paid product.

Demo: a hosted demo instance with sample reels and publishing disabled, plus a
2-minute screen recording.


7. PAID PROVIDERS FOR BUYERS (LATER / SECONDARY)
------------------------------------------------
The provider system already supports this: each capability (text, image, voice,
video) is a plug-in that can be switched on in Settings -> AI providers.
For buyers, use "bring your own API key": they enter their own keys for paid services
(e.g. Claude, ElevenLabs, Kling), pay the provider directly, and your cost stays zero.
